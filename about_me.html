A Recent Trip

One of my favorite getaways from the past few years was a trip to Marrakech. I enjoyed the warm weather, the food, and the energy of the city. It was a nice break from my normal routine and gave me time to relax, walk around, and enjoy Moroccan culture.

My Favorite Restaurant

One of my favorite restaurants is La Sqala in Casablanca. I like it because the atmosphere feels very Moroccan, the food is traditional, and it is a nice place to go with family or friends.

Visit their website here: La Sqala

My Favorite Food

One of my favorite foods is couscous. I like to eat it with vegetables, chickpeas, and meat, especially when it is served family-style. It is one of those meals that feels comforting and traditional.
